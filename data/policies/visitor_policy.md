# Metro General Hospital Visitor Guidelines & Hours (POL-VIS-001, v2.1)

## SECTION 1: GENERAL VISITING HOURS
Visiting hours across all inpatient adult medical and surgical units are from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. A maximum of two (2) visitors are permitted at the patient's bedside at any single time. All visitors must check in at the Main Lobby security desk, present government-issued photo identification, and wear a visitor pass badge at all times.

## SECTION 2: INTENSIVE CARE UNIT (ICU) VISITING RULES
In the Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and Coronary Care Unit (CCU), visiting hours are restricted to:
- Morning Session: 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM
- Evening Session: 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM
Only immediate family members aged 14 and older are permitted in the ICU. No fresh flowers, potted plants, or outside food items are permitted in critical care rooms to protect immune-compromised patients. Quiet hours are strictly maintained between 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM.

## SECTION 3: PEDIATRIC & MATERNITY WARDS
Parents and designated legal guardians have 24/7 bedside access in the Pediatric Ward. One parent may stay overnight in designated family sleeping chairs. In the Labor and Delivery unit, up to two designated support persons are permitted throughout labor and delivery.

## SECTION 4: INFECTION CONTROL & MASKS
Visitors displaying active signs of respiratory infection, fever, or gastrointestinal illness are strictly prohibited from entering patient care wings. Surgical masks must be worn in oncology and bone marrow transplant units. Hand hygiene must be performed at room entry and exit.
