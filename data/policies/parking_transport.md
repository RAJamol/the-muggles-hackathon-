# Metro General Hospital Parking & Transportation Policy (POL-TRN-002, v1.4)

## SECTION 1: PARKING LOCATIONS AND RATES
Metro General Hospital provides two primary parking facilities for patients and guests:
- Parking Garage A (Emergency & Surgical Pavilion): Located off Main Street Entrance. Open 24/7.
- Parking Garage B (Ambulatory Clinic & Medical Office Building): Located on East Campus Avenue. Open 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Standard Parking Rates:
- First 30 minutes: Free of charge.
- 31 minutes to 2 hours: $4.00.
- 2 hours to 4 hours: $7.00.
- Daily Maximum (over 4 hours): $12.00.

## SECTION 2: PARKING VALIDATION FOR PATIENTS
Inpatient admissions receive a complimentary 7-day family parking pass upon request at the Nursing Station. Outpatient surgery patients, oncology chemotherapy patients, and dialysis patients receive full 100% parking validation at their respective clinic reception counters.

## SECTION 3: VALET PARKING SERVICES
Valet drop-off is available at the Main Entrance circle drive from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. The fee is a flat $8.00 per vehicle. Patients with valid state-issued handicap placards receive complimentary valet service.

## SECTION 4: FREE COURTESY SHUTTLE
A free campus courtesy shuttle operates every 15 minutes between the East Campus Metro Rail Transit Station and the Hospital Main Pavilion between 6:30 AM and 9:00 PM on weekdays.
