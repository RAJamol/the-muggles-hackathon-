# Metro General Hospital Patient Discharge & Surgery Readiness (POL-DIS-003, v3.0)

## SECTION 1: DISCHARGE CRITERIA & TIMING
Standard hospital discharge time is 11:00 AM. Prior to discharge, the following milestones must be achieved and documented in the electronic medical record:
1. Attending physician has signed the electronic discharge summary.
2. Vital signs are stable within normal limits for at least 6 consecutive hours.
3. Patient has successfully tolerated solid foods and oral liquids without nausea.
4. Pain is adequately managed on oral medications.
5. Patient or designated representative has verbalized understanding of home wound care and medication schedules.

## SECTION 2: SURGICAL READINESS & PRE-OP FASTING (NPO)
For all outpatient and inpatient surgical procedures requiring sedation or general anesthesia:
- Solid food, chewing gum, mints, and milk products must be ceased at least 8 hours prior to scheduled surgery arrival (standard cutoff is 12:00 midnight).
- Clear liquids (water, apple juice without pulp, black coffee without milk/creamer) are permissible up to 2 hours before arrival time unless contraindicated by the surgeon.
- Essential medications (blood pressure, thyroid) should be swallowed with a single small sip of water on surgery morning, unless specifically paused by anesthesiology.

## SECTION 3: MANDATORY ADULT ESCORT
Patients receiving intravenous sedation, narcotics, or general anesthesia are legally prohibited from driving themselves home or utilizing public transit unaccompanied. An adult escort aged 18 or older must accompany the patient from the recovery suite.
